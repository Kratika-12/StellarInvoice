# Brand — StellarInvoice

StellarInvoice is a friendly creator tool for requesting and receiving XLM payments.

## Visual direction

- Light, playful, and approachable rather than corporate fintech.
- Soft lavender background with a warm coral primary action color.
- Rounded cards and controls, generous whitespace, subtle cool-toned shadows.
- Nunito-style rounded typography and concise, reassuring copy.
- Success uses mint green, pending uses warm yellow, and errors use calm orange-red.

## Interaction direction

- Primary actions are obvious and touch-friendly.
- Wallet and transaction states are always visible.
- Motion is brief, purposeful, and disabled when reduced motion is requested.
- Errors explain what happened and provide a recovery action.
